POWERFUL CAPABILITIES

Revolutionary Reading Analytics

Professional-grade eye tracking technology meets AI-powered comprehension assistance

👁️

Real-Time Eye Tracking

Advanced webcam-based gaze estimation using MediaPipe FaceMesh with 468 facial landmarks. Tracks your reading position at 30 FPS with sub-degree accuracy—no expensive hardware required.

🧠

AI-Powered Text Assistance

Integrated Llama 3.3 70B language model provides instant paragraph summarization and simplification. Get help understanding complex text with a single click—no interruption to your flow.

📊

Reading Behavior Analytics

Comprehensive metrics including words per minute (WPM), fixation duration, saccade patterns, and regression analysis. Understand your reading habits like never before.

🎯

Intelligent Fixation Detection

I-DT (Dispersion-Threshold Identification) algorithm automatically identifies which words and paragraphs you focus on. See exactly where your attention goes during reading.

🔥

Difficulty Heatmaps

Visual overlay highlighting challenging text sections based on your fixation patterns. Instantly identify where you struggled and optimize your learning strategy.

⚡

KDE Smoothing & Kalman Filtering

Production-grade signal processing with Kernel Density Estimation and auto-tuned Kalman filters. Eliminates jitter for smooth, accurate gaze tracking on any webpage.

GET STARTED

Up and Running in Minutes

Simple setup process with automatic calibration and tuning

1

Install the Extension

Add ReaRead to Chrome from the Web Store (or load unpacked from GitHub). The extension integrates seamlessly with any webpage you visit.

2

Run the Companion App

Download and run the Python companion app. It handles all the heavy lifting—eye tracking, machine learning inference, and gaze estimation—locally on your machine.

3

Quick Calibration

Follow the automatic calibration wizard (9 points, 20 seconds). The system builds a personalized gaze model and auto-tunes Kalman filters for optimal accuracy.

4

Start Reading!

Open any article or webpage. Watch real-time gaze tracking, get AI assistance on difficult paragraphs, and review your reading analytics when you're done.
