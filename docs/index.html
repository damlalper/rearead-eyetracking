Features

Professional eye tracking, now in your browser

Research-grade technology powered by computer vision, machine learning, and AI.

Real-time Gaze Tracking

Track your gaze at 30 FPS using MediaPipe FaceMesh with 468 facial landmarks. See exactly where you're looking on the page.

5-Mode AI Assistant

Gemini 2.0 Flash & Groq Llama 3.3 70B powered help: Summary, Vocabulary, Ask Questions, Key Points, and Audio Summary modes.

Reading Analytics

Comprehensive metrics including words per minute, fixation duration, regression patterns, and reading speed over time.

Smart Fixation Detection

I-DT algorithm with KDE smoothing identifies exactly which words and paragraphs you focus on most.

Difficulty Heatmaps

Visual overlay showing which parts of text were challenging based on your fixation patterns and re-reading behavior.

Advanced Signal Processing

Kalman filters with auto-tuning and KDE smoothing ensure accurate, jitter-free tracking even with head movement.

Learning Insights

Understand your reading behavior: identify patterns, track improvement, and optimize your learning strategy.

Privacy First

All processing happens locally on your machine. No data sent to servers. Your reading habits stay private.

Works Everywhere

Chrome extension works on any webpage: articles, PDFs, research papers, documentation, and more.

Hands-Free Gesture Control

Navigate with double blinks and head movements. Perfect for accessibility. State-based blink detection with 95%+ accuracy.

Auto-Read Mode

Automatic paragraph reading with dwell detection. Real-time translation to 8 languages. Text-to-speech with native accents.

Focus Mode & Pomodoro

Real-time distraction alerts when gaze goes out of bounds. Built-in Pomodoro timer (25 min work, 5 min break) with session tracking.

Gamification System

Level up (1-10), earn XP points, unlock badges, and build reading streaks. 90-day activity heatmap and personalized insights.

Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive reading metrics: Focus scores, reading speed trends, LLM usage charts, difficulty cloud, and streak tracking with Chart.js visualizations.

Setup

Get started in under 5 minutes

Simple installation with automatic calibration and intelligent tuning.

1

Install the Extension

Add ReaRead to Chrome from the GitHub repository. Load it as an unpacked extension. Works on any webpage you visit—articles, PDFs, documentation, and more.

2

Run Companion App

Download and run the Python companion app. It handles eye tracking and ML inference locally on your machine. Supports Windows, macOS, and Linux.

3

Quick Calibration

Follow the 9-point calibration wizard (takes 20 seconds). The system builds a personalized gaze estimation model using Ridge Regression trained on your facial features.

4

Start Reading

Open any article and watch real-time gaze tracking. Difficult paragraphs are automatically highlighted. Click for AI assistance, summaries, or simplifications.
